# Antioch Bible

God's Word in many languages, side by side, and it all works offline. A free Bible app by [Antioch Media](https://theantiochmedia.com).

## Download for Android

**[Download the latest APK](https://github.com/theantiochmedia/antioch-bible-releases/releases/latest/download/antioch-bible.apk)** on your phone, open it, and allow installing from your browser if Android asks. To update, install the new APK over the old one: your notes and progress are kept. All versions are under [Releases](../../releases).

## iPhone and computers

Use the web app at **https://bible.theantiochmedia.com**. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**: it opens like an app and works offline.

## What's inside

- The app in English, 简体中文, Bahasa Indonesia, 日本語 and 한국어
- Many translations side by side; download once and read offline
- Matthew Henry's commentary, Concise and Complete (optional download)
- Greek and Hebrew interlinear with lexicons and concordance (optional download)
- Reading plans, including First Steps for new believers, and Fast and pray
- Highlights, notes, bookmarks and memorization (also in pinyin and romaji)
- A Home screen you arrange yourself

This repository holds the app's releases. Scripture texts are used under the licences listed in the app (Profile → About).
